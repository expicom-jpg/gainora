# Gainora — investor readiness (optional financing)

**Status:** Internal preparation, not a decision to raise money, an investor offer, or a representation of existing traction. Do not share externally without founder approval and fact checking.

## Investment thesis
Gainora aims to become a customer-first Profit Intelligence ecosystem with three connected networks:
1. Customer Network: SaaS subscription, evidence-based savings recommendations.
2. Partner Network: recurring distribution and paid-subscription commission.
3. Supplier Network: verified alternatives and recurring commissions on eligible, paid customer purchases.

Customer value comes first; supplier fees never determine ranking. Separate supplier GMV from Gainora revenue. All projected network effects are hypotheses until supported by retention, conversion and unit economics.

## Optional financing decision gates

- Both founders agree on ownership, roles, time commitment, decision rights, vesting and leaver provisions in a signed founders' agreement.
- Product demonstrates secure tenant isolation, tested data imports, real recommendations, and clear operational ownership.
- Real customer data only after security, DPA, retention, backup/restore and access controls have passed review.
- Evidence of willingness to pay: pilots, paid conversions, churn and verified customer outcomes; clearly label synthetic demo data.
- Board/founders approve a 12–18 month use-of-funds plan, milestones and a minimum acceptable runway.
- Lawyer/accountant review Danish corporate structure, financing documents, employment and tax implications.

## Possible financing paths

- Bootstrap/customer revenue: maximum ownership retained, potentially slower.
- Small milestone-based angel round: reduced immediate dilution but subsequent funding risk.
- Larger 12–18 month angel/strategic round: faster execution, more dilution and investor rights.
- Relevant public innovation funding: verify current eligibility and deadlines before applications.

No fixed valuation or ownership offer until cap table, traction and legal structure are confirmed. Model dilution on a fully diluted basis including option pool, convertible instruments and future rounds.

## Milestone roadmap

**0–90 days (planning):** confirm founders' agreement and company structure; finish secure product, demonstrate reproducible customer-value analyses, produce 3-statement forecast and first paid-customer pipeline.

**3–6 months (planning):** validate onboarding, retention, CAC and paid conversion; test partner attribution and supplier matching with consent and verifiable data; quantify unit economics.

**6–18 months (planning):** scale only proven channels, automate settlement and reporting, improve retention and demonstrate a repeatable acquisition process.

Milestones are hypotheses and must be dated only after a founder-approved operating plan.

## Decision scorecard

Compare bootstrap vs investment based on: expected speed to product-market fit; realistic runway; dilution; governance/control; customer acquisition access; operational risk; ability to reach the next funding-independent milestone; founder welfare and cash-flow resilience.
