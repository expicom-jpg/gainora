# Gainora sales channel contribution model (illustrative)

**Status:** Planning assumptions, not audited financials or approved commission contracts. DKK/month excl. VAT.

## Base assumptions
- Subscription: DKK 2,495/customer/month; all 100 customers actively paying.
- Customer attribution mutually exclusive: Morten own 40; employed seller 40; external referral partners 20.
- Morten own marginal recurring commission: 8% on first 20; 10% on next 20; 12% on next 20; 14% on next 40; 16% above 100.
- Morten organization override: 2% on paid net subscription revenue of subordinate employed sellers only; **no override on external partner referrals**.
- Employee seller commission assumed 10% on own paid portfolio for illustration; actual commission policy requires approval and may be progressive.
- External partner referral recurring commission assumed 20% (unapproved illustrative rate). Review duration, caps, eligibility and contribution margin before contract.
- Platform and processing variable expense assumed 10% of subscription revenue; fixed operating expense 20,000/month.
- Fixed gross salaries: Morten 35,000; one employed seller 27,500. Employer burden placeholder 15% each. Not a substitute for pension, paid leave, insurance, payroll tax or local legal assessment.
- Excludes supplier-network revenue/commissions, founder salary, marketing growth spend, company tax, churn, bad debt, exit bonus funding and any other sales staff.

## Rules and risks
1. A paying customer belongs to exactly one commissionable acquisition channel at a time; record immutable attribution, effective dates, contract versions and any reassignment audit.
2. Reconcile against **collected** payments, excluding VAT, discounts, credits, chargebacks and refunds. Reverse commissions when underlying receipts reverse.
3. Distinguish Morten's own progressive commission from employee seller commission; do not automatically apply his 8–16% bands to other sellers.
4. No Head of Sales override on external partner referrals unless separately approved after margin review.
5. Establish a floor contribution margin per cohort and partner-channel payback threshold before offering perpetual 20% referrals.
6. Model churn, customer acquisition cost, onboarding and support costs, supplier-network revenues, and multi-year cash flows before final employment and partner agreements.
7. Treat 5% potential cash exit bonus as a contingent transaction liability, not a monthly subscription commission; define payer and waterfall in signed legal documentation.
8. Customer point system for exit milestones is distinct from cash commissions: direct organization customers = 1.0; partner referrals = 0.5; no double counting.

## Open decisions

- Actual sales executive commission schedule and target variable compensation.
- Minimum cash runway and customer retention before moving Morten from part-time to full-time.
- Fully loaded employment costs and acquisition cost by channel.
