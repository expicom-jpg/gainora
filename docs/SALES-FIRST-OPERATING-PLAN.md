# Gainora — Sales-first operating and capital allocation plan

**Status:** Internal operating proposal. Founder indicates no salary is needed for them at present. This is not a waiver of any legal salary entitlement, nor authorization to hire, spend or issue equity.

## Priority
Grow verified paying SaaS revenue before founder compensation or speculative supplier-network expansion. Maintain product security, customer value and compliance as non-negotiable launch gates.

## Capital allocation order
1. Minimum viable security, legal/data protection, reliable onboarding, payments and evidence-backed product.
2. Founder-led customer discovery, pilot conversion and paid sales.
3. CRM, lead attribution, follow-up automation, pipeline stages and conversion analytics.
4. Hire/contract sales capacity only when gross margin, CAC payback and cash runway justify fully loaded compensation.
5. Sales commission ledger and role-scoped dashboards.
6. Broader supplier network and international expansion only after repeatable SaaS acquisition is demonstrated.

## Compensation and hiring
- Founder salary assumed **0 DKK for planning purposes only**, subject to formal founder decision and personal financial sustainability.
- Sales Executive fixed salary 27,500 DKK/month gross.
- Head of Sales fixed salary 35,000 DKK/month gross.
- Progressive marginal portfolio commission 8/10/12/14/16% at customer bands 1–20, 21–40, 41–60, 61–100, 101+.
- Head of Sales receives additional 2% on paid SaaS subscriptions from assigned subordinate sellers, excluding own portfolio.
- Employer costs, pension, holiday, external partner commissions and team override must all be included in unit economics.
- No hiring commitments until approved cash forecast and legal review.

## 90-day commercial plan (targets, not actual traction)
**Days 1–30:** define ICP and offer; ensure pilot onboarding, product safety, CRM funnel, sales scripts and demo; create a measurable lead list with lawful outreach.
**Days 31–60:** run pilots, capture verified savings/feedback, improve conversion and obtain first paid contracts where product readiness permits.
**Days 61–90:** measure retention, acquisition cost, sales cycle and contribution margin; decide on first seller/Head of Sales commitment using forecast and pipeline evidence.

## Sales scorecard (weekly)
- Leads sourced, qualified leads, meetings booked, demos, pilots started, paid conversions.
- Conversion rates by stage, average sales cycle, price realized, MRR/ARR.
- Gross margin after variable platform costs and sales/partner commissions.
- Churn, retention, customer value realized, CAC and CAC payback.
- Cash runway, collections, receivables and hiring capacity.

## Hiring decision gate
Approve role only when the downside cash forecast supports at least 6 months of fully loaded payroll and operating expenses, plus agreed liquidity reserve; target sales pipeline and expected payback must be evidenced, not assumed. This 6-month threshold is an internal proposal.

## Funding decision
Update 12/18-month fundraising budget from a two-founder-salary scenario to scenarios with zero salary for one founder, and explicit sales hiring, acquisition and product/compliance spending. Compare bootstrap, revenue-led hiring, and an optional smaller milestone-based financing round. Do not approach investors or make commitments without founder approval.
