# Gainora 2.0 Cost Model

## Pilot target
Five companies with controlled low-volume usage.

## Initial monthly estimate
- GitHub: 0 DKK initially
- Database/auth/storage: 0-200 DKK
- Hosting: 0-150 DKK
- AI/API: 100-300 DKK
- Transactional email: 0-100 DKK
- Monitoring/backups/misc: 50-150 DKK

Working pilot budget: 500 DKK/month.
Practical early range: approximately 150-900 DKK/month depending on plan choices and usage.

## Cost principle
Do not buy a paid plan before a free or lower-cost tier becomes an actual blocker.
